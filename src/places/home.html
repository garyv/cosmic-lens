Explore the universe through stunning space telescope photography. Dive into the cosmos and discover the beauty of distant galaxies, nebulae, and star clusters.

Use the navigation on the side to reveal photos captured by NASA.

These photos are orderred by light years — the distance light travels in a year marks both their distance from Earth and how far back in time we are seeing them.

Each photo comes with a detailed description of the celestial phenomena depicted and controls to zoom in for a closer look.

This is a web app written in TypeScript and React by Gary Von Schilling and using photos and data provided by NASA. Souce code avaiable on GitHub.
