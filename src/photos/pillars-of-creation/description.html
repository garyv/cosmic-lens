The Pillars of Creation are towering structures of interstellar gas and dust located in the Eagle Nebula (M16)

The pillars are part of a star-forming region where new stars are being born within the dense clouds of gas and dust.

The name "Pillars of Creation" reflects the idea that these structures are sites of ongoing star formation, symbolizing the creation of new celestial bodies in the universe.
