The Pillars of Creation are towering structures of interstellar gas and dust located in the Eagle Nebula (M16).

When a clump of gas and dust inside a pillar gathers enough mass, it collapses under its own gravity to create a star. The bright red orbs just outside one of the pillars are newborn stars.

The wavy crimson edges that look like lava are shock waves. Stars still forming inside the pillars, only a few hundred thousand years old, fire supersonic jets that slam into the surrounding gas.
